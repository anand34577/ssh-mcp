# Welcome to SSH MCP

SSH MCP lets your AI assistant work on your servers for you. Ask in plain words, like *"why is my website slow?"* or *"how much disk space is left?"*, and your AI signs in to the server, runs the right commands, and explains what it found.

It works with **Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf**, and any other app that supports MCP (the standard way AI apps plug into tools).

## Why people trust it

- **Your passwords stay on your computer.** The AI never sees them, not even once.
- **You decide what the AI may do.** Choose full access or *look-only* for each server.
- **It checks every server's identity** so nobody can pretend to be your server.
- **No analytics, no tracking.** It never sends anything about you anywhere. See [Privacy](Privacy).
- **Free and open source.** Anyone can read every line of code.
